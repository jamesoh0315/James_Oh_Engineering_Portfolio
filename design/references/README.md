# Inspiration library (Step 1)

Working files for the design overhaul. Nothing here is part of the site build —
it lives outside src/ and public/ on purpose.

## What to drop in this folder

**8–15 screenshots.** Fewer than 8 and there's not enough signal to find a pattern;
more than 15 and the common thread turns to mush.

- **Full hero sections or full-page captures**, not small crops. Context matters —
  how much empty space surrounds a headline is as informative as the headline.
- **PNG or JPG.** Any filename is fine.
- Include a couple you *dislike* if they're instructive. Knowing what to avoid is
  as useful as knowing what to chase.

## Also fill in NOTES.md

One line per screenshot saying what specifically caught your eye. This is the part
that can't be inferred from the image — you might have saved something for its
typography while hating its colour, and there's no way to tell those apart by
looking.

## A caution on Dribbble

Dribbble shots are often non-functional mockups: fake data, impossible layouts, no
mobile state, no consideration of real content length. They're good for mood and
colour, unreliable as structural blueprints. Pinterest boards of *real shipped
sites* are more useful for layout decisions.

## Live sites

If a reference is a real site rather than a screenshot, put the URL in NOTES.md as
well. A live URL lets the actual fonts, colour values, and spacing scale be read
directly, which is more precise than eyeballing a picture.
